Student Talent Discovery & Development Management System (STMS)



📌 Project Overview

The Student Talent Discovery & Development Management System (STMS) is a web-based system being developed for INTANGO TSS to help the school discover, develop, assess, manage, and showcase the talents and abilities of its students.

The system provides a centralized platform for managing student interests, skills, ideas, practical challenges, projects, evidence, mentor observations, talent assessments, feedback, achievements, and development opportunities.

The main purpose of STMS is to help the school move from simply saying:

"We think this student is talented."

to having clear evidence of:

"Here is what the student is interested in, the challenge they completed, the idea they submitted, the project they developed, the evidence of their work, the mentor's observation, their talent assessment, and how they can be supported further."

🎯 Project Goal

STMS is designed to support continuous student talent discovery and development.

The system follows this general process:

Student Interest
       ↓
Idea or Challenge
       ↓
Activity
       ↓
Project

This allows student talent to be identified through actual activities, projects, challenges, evidence, and observed performance rather than assumptions.

📊 Talent Assessment

STMS provides a Talent Assessment Score out of 100 based on demonstrated talent and performance.

Assessment Criterion	Maximum
Creativity & Innovation	15
Problem Solving	20
Technical / Practical Ability	20
Communication	10
Teamwork & Collaboration	10
Initiative	10
Learning Ability	10
Consistency & Commitment	5
Total	100

The system automatically calculates the total score.

🏆 Talent Classification
Score	Classification
85–100	Exceptional Potential
70–84	High Potential
55–69	Developing Potential
40–54	Emerging Talent
0–39	Needs Development
